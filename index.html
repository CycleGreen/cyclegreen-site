01 / How it works

A record that doesn't stop
at planting.

From the first photo to the next survival check. Every step leaves a trace.

  1. 01

    Record

    Communities and field teams record work with photos and location, even offline.

  2. 02

    Review

    A person checks the evidence. Every decision has a reason.

  3. 03

    Revisit

    Survival checks months later show what actually took root.

  4. 04

    Release

    Benefits are released in stages. Communities can see their own status.

Stage payments follow agreed survival checks. CycleGreen records their status; it does not guarantee payment.

03 / Clear boundaries

What CycleGreen
is not

Accountability starts with being clear about our limits.

  • Not a payment provider or a guarantee of payment.
  • Not proof of long-term ecological impact on its own.
  • Not built for a single funder.
